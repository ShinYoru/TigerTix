Glorietta 4

5th Level Glorietta 4
Ayala Center, Makati City

Glorietta Cinemas in Makati offers a modern moviegoing experience with stylish interiors and advanced technology. Its spacious lobby features one of the largest immersive LED screens in the country, creating an exciting atmosphere as soon as you enter. Inside the theaters, Dolby Atmos sound and laser projection provide stunning visuals and clear audio, while the A-Luxe cinemas feature comfortable motorized recliners for a more luxurious viewing experience.
